# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background or identity. We are committed to a welcoming,
respectful, and constructive environment.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and
publishing others' private information without permission.

## Sensitive subject matter

This project concerns mental health and suicide. Please engage with the topic
respectfully. Content must remain educational and must not be framed as medical
advice or individual risk assessment.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer
via the repository's contact channels. Maintainers will review and respond as
appropriate, and may remove or reject contributions that violate this code.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
